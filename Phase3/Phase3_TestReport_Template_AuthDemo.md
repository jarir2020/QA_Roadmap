## Theory: a report describes evidence and its limits

A result is meaningful only with its context: application version, browser engine/version, operating system, viewport, input method, and setup. Report each quality dimension separately because a page may pass layout checks while failing keyboard access or browser-specific behavior.

Screenshots show a visual state at one point in time; they do not prove that controls work, that content is accessible, or that the system behaves correctly at other sizes. Link evidence to a specific observation and include the steps that produced it. Mark untested combinations and unresolved risks so readers do not interpret a partial sample as universal coverage.
